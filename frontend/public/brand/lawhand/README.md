# LawHand brand assets

The production identity uses a lowercase `lawhand` wordmark, Carbon and Parchment as its foundation, and Cobalt only for action and state.

- Primary typeface: Manrope, with Inter and Arial fallbacks.
- Primary message: “The whole matter, in hand.”
- Product name in prose: `LawHand`; visual wordmark: `lawhand`.
- The lowercase `a` application mark is for favicons, app tiles, and compact product surfaces. Do not substitute an `LH` monogram.
- `lawhand-mark-512-transparent.png` is the raster mark for third-party consent
  screens and app listings (Google OAuth, Microsoft Entra, Teams) that require a
  square PNG/JPG and reject SVG. Corners are transparent; the carbon field stays
  opaque because the letterforms are ivory and need it for contrast. Do not make
  the whole background transparent. `icon-512.png` is the opaque PWA/app-tile
  variant and is intentionally left square.
- Avoid legal clichés, decorative scripts, gradients, gold effects, and ornamental seals.

Open `index.html` to review the core identity and asset treatments.
