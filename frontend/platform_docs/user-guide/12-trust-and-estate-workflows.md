# Trust, estate & probate workflows

The [Estate Portfolio](/plugins/trust-estate/estates) organizes probate estates and trust administrations. Each estate record provides structured operational tracking alongside the matter and source documents.

## Establish the estate record

Confirm the estate or trust name, type, status, grantor or decedent, jurisdiction, domicile, date of death, representative type, court, case number, and known values. Use source documents for dates and legal names.

## Open a probate from the Probate tab

For a North Dakota estate, the Probate tab does the opening work:

1. **Send probate intake** opens the matter's paperwork drawer with the probate questionnaire. Clients answer it in the portal, on the paper copy the office prints and mails, or over the phone while staff type into the Facts form — the questions are the same.
2. **Pull from client questionnaire** brings the client's answers into the estate's facts without overwriting what staff already entered.
3. **Save facts and determine track** works out which proceeding applies — informal probate of a will, informal appointment in intestacy, a formal proceeding after three years, a small-estate affidavit, or no North Dakota proceeding — and says why, with the statute. Correct a fact to change the answer.
4. **Court forms** lists the NDPC forms the track files, with the pages to print for each. The court's packet is installed into your templates as a draft the first time you open the tab; an attorney publishes it in Templates, then **Generate filled packet** fills every form from the estate record for review.
5. **Deadline clock** turns the date of death, appointment date, and first publication of the notice to creditors into the statutory deadlines, optionally as matter tasks. Re-running after a date changes moves open deadlines and leaves completed ones alone.

For a formal proceeding the court publishes no forms; the tab lists the pleadings the firm drafts and uploads as its own templates.

## Client estate inventory

Once the personal representative is appointed (or you open it early from the Probate facts), the client's portal shows an **Estate inventory** tab where they list what the estate owns in plain words with a rough value and an optional statement or photo. Each item waits on the estate's Assets tab until staff verify or reject it; only verified items count toward the inventory.

## Work through the estate tabs

Depending on the record, the detail view tracks:

- fiduciaries and appointment details;
- beneficiaries and interests;
- estate assets and valuations;
- creditor claims and disposition;
- deadlines and filing status;
- distributions, approvals, payment status, and check references; and
- an activity history.

Assign one primary fiduciary only when the legal documents support that designation. Keep estimates distinguishable from appraised or filed values.

## Deadlines and claims

Record the source and status of each deadline. Verify publication, notice, inventory, tax, account, and distribution dates under the governing jurisdiction and orders. For claims, preserve the claimant, type, amount, received date, priority, and disposition.

## Distributions

Before marking a distribution approved or paid, confirm beneficiary identity, authority, available funds, reserves, tax and claim implications, and required receipts. The workflow tracks activity; it does not determine whether a distribution is lawful.

Use [Trust Accounting](/trust) for authorized client-fund ledger activity. Do not treat estate asset values in the workflow as a substitute for trust or bank reconciliation.
