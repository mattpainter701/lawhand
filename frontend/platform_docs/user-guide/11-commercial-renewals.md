# Commercial contract renewals

The [Contract Renewal Tracker](/plugins/commercial/renewals) lists the contracts your team is watching, soonest renewal first, so a notice window is never missed. It is part of the commercial add-on; if you cannot open it, see [Add-on module management](/guide/add-on-module-management).

## Read the tracker

Each row shows the **Contract**, **Vendor**, **Annual Value**, **Owner**, **Renewal Date**, **Cancel-By** deadline, whether it **Auto-Renew**s, its **Urgency**, and its **Status**.

Urgency counts the days until the renewal date:

| Urgency | Renewal date is |
| --- | --- |
| **Urgent** | 13 days away or less |
| **High** | 14 to 30 days away |
| **Medium** | 31 to 60 days away |
| **Low** | 61 to 90 days away |

Contracts further out show no urgency yet. Urgency follows the renewal date, not the cancel-by deadline, so always check the **Cancel-By** column too: the deadline to give notice usually comes well before the renewal date.

## Add a renewal

Take every value from the executed agreement or another verified source.

1. Select **Add Renewal**.
2. Enter the **Contract Name**, the **Vendor** (use the legal counterparty name if it differs from a brand name), the **Annual Value**, and the **Business Owner** who decides whether to renew.
3. Set the **Renewal Date** and the **Cancel-By Deadline**, the last day notice of non-renewal can be given.
4. Tick **Auto-renewal enabled** if the contract renews unless someone gives notice.
5. Leave the **Status** as **Pending** until a decision is made.
6. Use **Notes** for the next action and anything a reviewer needs, such as "Request pricing by 1 March; notice must go by courier to the address in section 14."
7. Select **Add Renewal** to save it.

> [!WARNING]
> Work out the cancel-by deadline from the contract itself: whether it counts calendar or business days, how notice must be delivered, and whether an amendment changed the term. The tracker records the date you enter; it does not read the contract.

## Keep renewals moving

- Review the tracker weekly, starting with **Urgent** and **High** rows.
- Give every row an owner and a concrete next action: gather usage data, request pricing, send a non-renewal notice, or route an amendment for approval.
- Change the **Status** as decisions are made: **Approved** once renewal is approved, **Renewed** once it is done, or **Cancelled** when notice was given.
- Select **Edit Renewal** to update dates after a renewal, and **Save Changes**.
- **Delete** removes a row permanently after you confirm **Delete renewal**. Prefer marking it **Cancelled** or **Renewed** so the decision stays on record.

Renewals also appear on the [Calendar](/calendar) as **Renewal** items. Store the executed agreement and any notices in the relevant [matter](/matters). The tracker summarizes obligations; it does not replace the contract.

Escalate ambiguous notice language, unusually high value, data-security changes, sanctions concerns, or a missed notice window to the responsible attorney or contract owner.

## Troubleshooting

| What you notice | Likely cause | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Contract name is required." | The name is empty | Enter the contract name and save again. |
| A contract has no urgency badge | Its renewal date is more than 90 days away, or it has none | Check the renewal date; still watch the cancel-by deadline. |
| The tracker will not open | The commercial add-on is not active for your firm | Ask your administrator. |
