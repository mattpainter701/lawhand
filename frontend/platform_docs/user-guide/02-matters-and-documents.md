# Matters & documents

The matter is the home for legal work. From it you see status and risk, the people involved, the next deadlines, the documents, the email, and what the matter has cost so far. This chapter walks through finding a matter, touring the record, and the everyday document and correspondence tasks.

## Find your matters

Open [My Matters](/matters). The page has two parts: **My Matters**, the matters assigned to you, and **All accessible matters** further down, every matter you are allowed to see.

![The My Matters list with the status tabs, keyword filter, Columns button, Board and List toggle, and the Working on this toggle](/guide-assets/matters-list.webp "My Matters in list view")

1. **Status tabs.** **All**, **Open**, **Active**, **Pending**, and **Closed**, each with a count.
2. **Filter by keyword.** Narrows the list by matter name, number, client, attorney, or practice area.
3. **Columns.** Choose which columns appear on this device. The everyday set is matter, client, responsible attorney, status, and next deadline; tick **Originating attorney**, **Practice area**, **Open date**, **Risk**, or **Cloud folder** to add them, or select **Show all**. Drag the edge of a column heading to resize it, and select a heading to sort by it.
4. **Board / List.** Switch between the list and the lifecycle board.
5. **Work on this.** Marks you as currently working the matter so colleagues can see it. It does not change the matter's status.

Under the heading, a red link such as **2 matters need attention** opens a filtered view. **Needs attention** covers threatened matters, overdue or due-today deadlines, and open or active matters nobody has updated for more than 14 days. An amber line lists matters due tomorrow.

> [!TIP]
> The filters, sort, and view are part of the page address, so you can bookmark "my matters by next deadline" or send it to a colleague as a link.

If you have many assigned matters, the list loads them in pages. Select **Load more** at the bottom to see the rest; until then, search and filters apply only to the matters already loaded, and the page says so.

### Use the board

Select **Board** to see your matters as cards in four lifecycle columns: **Open**, **Active**, **Pending**, and **Closed**.

![The lifecycle board with the drag handle on a matter card and the Closed column](/guide-assets/matters-board.webp "The board: drag a card to change its status")

1. Drag a card by its handle to another column to change the matter's status.
2. Dropping a card on **Closed** does not close the matter immediately. It opens the same **Close this matter** check you get from the matter record, so unbilled work or client trust money is never stranded by accident. See [Close a matter](#close-a-matter).

### All accessible matters

Below your own matters, **All accessible matters** lists every matter you can see. Status cards show totals for **Open**, **Active**, **Pending**, **Closed**, and **Critical Risk**. Use the status, practice area, and engagement filters, or **Search matters** by matter name, number, client, or organization.

### Open a matter by its number

Every matter has a short number, such as `HARL0001`, shown in a badge you can click to copy. You can also open a matter by typing its number in the address, for example `/matters/HARL0001`.

## Open a new matter

1. On [My Matters](/matters), select **New Matter**.
2. Enter the **Matter Title**, for example "Smith v. Acme Corp". Add a **Description**, **Practice Area**, **Status**, and **Open date** if you have them.
3. Under **Engagement**, choose **New engagement** if you will send the fee agreement and paperwork from the matter, or **Already engaged** to record an existing engagement without sending anything.
4. Choose the client. Search for an existing contact, or select **+ Create new contact**, fill in the name, email, and mobile number, and select **Create & Select Contact**.
5. Choose the **Attorney of Record** and **Partner Attorney**, and add any **Additional Team Members**.
6. For litigation, open **Litigation / Court Details (optional)** and fill in the represented side, counterparty, jurisdiction, case number, court, and judge.
7. Select **Open Matter**. The matter record opens, and a new engagement shows **Start this case** on its Overview so you can send the client paperwork.

## Tour of a matter

![A matter record with the Matter settings and Close matter buttons, the section tabs, and the Quick Actions panel](/guide-assets/matter-overview.webp "The matter record's Overview")

The top of the record answers "whose matter is this and who owns it": the matter number, client, responsible attorney, partner, status, risk, practice area, and case number, plus the trust balance and how much of the budget is used.

1. **Matter settings** opens the less frequent sections: **People** (the team and the caption [Parties](#define-caption-parties)), **Workflow**, **Correspondence**, **Chat**, and **Settings**.
2. **Close matter** runs the close check. On a closed matter the button becomes **Reopen matter**.
3. The everyday sections: **Overview**, **Activity** (timeline, notes, and logged communications), **Documents**, **Client Portal**, and **Billing**.
4. **Quick Actions**: **Log Time**, **Email Client**, **Add Task**, **Start Chat** (an assistant conversation scoped to this matter), **Add Note**, **Research** (the matter's [research workspace](#collaborate-on-research)), and **Brief Check** (a [brief review](#check-a-litigation-brief)).

Below the quick actions, **Next work** shows **Key Dates** (hearings, filings, depositions, and deadlines) and a **To-Do** list. Tick the circle beside an item to complete it, or select **Manage matter tasks** to see everything on the Tasks page.

**Customize view** hides sections and fields you do not need. It applies to every matter you open on this device; hidden values stay available under **Edit**, and the matter's identity and alerts always stay visible. **Edit** changes the matter's details. Each section has its own address: on a matter's documents the address ends in `?tab=documents`, so you can bookmark or share it.

> [!TIP]
> Use the **Guide** button while you are on a matter: it opens the part of this chapter for the section you are looking at.

## Keep the file coherent

Within a matter:

- use the Overview for the current posture and important facts;
- keep parties and contacts attached to the matter rather than repeating details in notes;
- upload documents to the matter so retrieval and history have the correct scope;
- use tasks and deadlines for work that needs an owner; and
- record meaningful changes in the matter instead of relying on a private side list.

## Define caption parties

For litigation matters, keep the firm's posture separate from the people or organizations named in the caption:

- **Represented Side / Our Role** describes the firm's side of the case, such as plaintiff's counsel or defense counsel. It is part of the matter's details.
- **Client** identifies the contact the firm represents. A client is not automatically a plaintiff; the client may be a defendant, petitioner, respondent, insurer, or another interested person.
- **Plaintiff** and **Defendant** in the **Parties** panel identify the named civil-action parties that document Smart Fill uses.
- **Counterparty Summary** is a quick general label for the opposing side. It is not a substitute for adding the named plaintiff and defendant contacts.

To add a caption party:

1. On the matter, select **Matter settings**, then **People**.
2. In the **Parties** panel, select **Add Party**.
3. Choose the **Contact** and the **Role** exactly as the pleading names it.
4. Tick **Primary for this role** when a template needs a single plaintiff or defendant name; otherwise the first listed contact is used.
5. Select **Add Party**.

A matter can have several plaintiffs and defendants. Use petitioner and respondent for petition-based proceedings rather than relabeling those parties to fill a plaintiff or defendant field.

## Work with documents

Open the matter's **Documents** section.

![The Case Documents section with Upload Document, Attach template, the search box, and the documents in progress list](/guide-assets/matter-documents.webp "A matter's documents")

1. **Upload Document** adds a file.
2. **Attach template** prepares a document from an approved Template Studio template.
3. **Search filename or description** filters the list, and also searches inside the documents' text.
4. **Documents in progress** lists drafts you started from a template, with **Resume** and **Discard**.

Before uploading, confirm that the document belongs to the matter on screen, and give it a filename a colleague will recognize. Avoid names such as `scan1.pdf` or `final-final.docx`.

### Upload a document

1. Select **Upload Document**.
2. Choose the **File**.
3. Pick a **Category** (pleading, contract, evidence, correspondence, or other) and add an optional **Description**.
4. Choose a **Folder**, or leave it **Unfiled (matter root)**. The file is written to the matching folder in your firm's cloud storage, so the cloud folder mirrors what you see here.
5. Select **Upload**.

### Organize with folders and tags

- **New Folder** creates a folder; the **Folders** rail on the left shows each folder with its document count.
- Drag a document row onto a folder to file it, or use **Move or copy…** above the list. Moving keeps the document's cloud storage path.
- Select **Tag** on a document to add tags, and use the **Tags** filter to show only tagged documents.
- Choose **Folder** or **Detailed** to switch between a folder view and the full table, and sort with **Newest first**, name, or size.

### Search inside a matter's documents

The search box also looks inside the documents' text once you type three or more characters. Matching passages appear under **Found inside documents** with an **Open** link; scanned pages read by OCR are included. The index follows each document's exact bytes, is built shortly after a document is first read, and is removed with the document. A passage is the document's own words, not a matter record: open the document before relying on it.

### Preview a document and read its details

Select **Preview** on a document row to read it without downloading. In the preview, open **Read details from a document** and select **Find details** to have LawHand look for supported information, such as names and dates, in the document. If the document was generated from a known template, choose its **Printed form** and select **Read against the printed form** to compare the answers with the original field locations.

Reading a scan can take several minutes. Keep the page open; if a timeout or connection error appears, retry when the connection is available. Review each proposed value against its source before accepting it. Reading alone does not change the matter's details.

### Share a document with the client

The **Client Portal** column shows whether the client can see each document:

- **Private** — only your firm can see it. Select it to share.
- **Shared with client** — visible in the client's portal. Select it to make the document private again.
- **Available to signing client** — not shared, but the person signing a packet that includes it can open it.
- **Release locked** — an assistant revision, which needs a separate approval before it can be released.

> [!WARNING]
> Sharing is immediate. Check that the document is final and appropriate for the client before you share it, and never share internal notes, other parties' materials, or draft strategy.

### Documents prepared from templates

When your firm's templates can be filled from the matter, a banner such as **2 documents ready to review, 88% filled from this matter** appears. Select **Review and save** to open one in the Prepare flow, check every value, and save it to the matter. Drafts you start but do not finish appear under **Documents in progress**; select **Resume** to continue or **Discard** to throw the draft away. See [Template Studio & e-signature](/guide/document-automation-and-esignature) for the full Prepare flow.

Generated content is a draft until a qualified person reviews and approves it.

### Edit a document in Word or Google Docs

LawHand does not have its own editor. A Word document (DOCX) stored in your firm's Microsoft 365 or Google storage is edited in Word or Google Docs, and the edits come back into LawHand as the same document's current version.

1. On the document's row, select **Open in Word** (Word for the web) or **Word app** (Word on your computer). For a file in Google Drive, select **Open in Google Docs**. Google Docs edits the file as a Word document, so it stays a DOCX.
2. The row shows **Being edited in Word by** *name* **since** *time*, so colleagues know the document is open. The note stays for 12 hours, and opening the document again renews it.
3. Make your changes. Word and Google Docs save as you go.
4. Come back to LawHand. The edits are brought back automatically when you return to the tab you opened the document from. You or a colleague can also select **Bring back changes** at any time.

Each time edits come back, the document keeps its name, folder, tags, and sharing. The change appears on the matter timeline as **Edits brought back**, and search reads the new text shortly afterwards.

No Microsoft 365 or Google access at hand? Select **Download**, edit the copy on your computer, then select **Upload revised** and choose the edited file. LawHand refuses the upload if someone changed the document in Word or Google Docs after it was last saved in LawHand. Select **Bring back changes** first, so no one's edits are lost.

Approved, filed, and signed documents, and documents out for signature, are never overwritten. If one of them is changed in Word or Google Docs, its row shows **Changed outside LawHand** with the reason. Restore the file in Word or Google Docs, or save the edited copy as a new document. Assistant drafts and revisions keep their own review; bring their edits back from the review task.

### Revise a document with the assistant

For a Word (DOCX) document, select **Revise with assistant** on its row. The revision opens on its own page with the document's saved revision history, so you can compare the source and the reviewed output, approve or reject a revision, and prepare a replacement for an e-signature request. Assistant revisions are **Release locked** until they go through the separate release approval, and **Back to matter documents** returns you to the list.

## Correspondence and matter email

The matter's **Correspondence** section keeps the email that belongs to the file with the rest of the record. Open it from **Matter settings**. It works two ways, and your firm may have either or both.

![The Correspondence section with Scan now, the matter's forwarding address, and an email awaiting review with File + create task](/guide-assets/matter-correspondence.webp "Correspondence under Matter settings")

1. **Correspondence** is one of the Matter settings sections.
2. **Scan now** archives matching messages from the connected mailbox you choose (**Outlook / 365** or **Gmail**).
3. The matter's forwarding address, with **Copy**, **Rotate**, and **Disable**.
4. **File + create task** files a forwarded email and creates the task its subject asked for.

### Capture from a connected mailbox

1. Select **Rules** to choose what is archived:
   - **Enable automatic background capture for this matter** keeps capturing without a manual scan.
   - **Capture emails involving the matter's parties (client, attorney, etc.)** matches the **Tracked email addresses**, which come from the matter's contacts and parties.
   - **Case / court numbers** captures messages whose subject or preview contains one of the numbers. The matter's case number is included automatically.
2. Select **Save rules**.
3. Choose the mailbox (**Outlook / 365** or **Gmail**) and select **Scan now**. A message such as "Scanned 40 email(s) — captured 3 new, 37 already on file" confirms the result.

To change the tracked addresses, update the contact or matter party they come from. An address left on the list keeps pulling mail into the matter. Messages are saved in full as `.eml` files, and threads are grouped as **Conversation · N messages**.

### Forward mail to the matter

1. Under **Forward email to this matter**, select **Create address**, then **Copy**.
2. Forward a message to that address, add it as a BCC recipient, or give it to a sender who needs to submit mail to this matter.

The address deliberately contains no client name or matter number. Treat it as an unlisted intake address and share it only with people who should be able to put mail into this queue. Select **Rotate** if it was shared too broadly (the old address stops working immediately, so update any forwarding rules), or **Disable** to stop accepting mail for the matter.

### Review what arrives

**Nothing files itself.** Forwarded mail waits under **Emails awaiting review**. For each message:

1. Check the sender, subject, date, and preview. The sender and the contents are untrusted until you verify them.
2. If the subject starts with `[TASK]`, `[REVIEW]`, or `[DEADLINE]`, a tag box shows the task it will create. Check the **Task title** and **Due date**; a deadline cannot be filed without a date.
3. Select **File to matter** (or **File + create task**, **review**, or **deadline**). This stores the original message and its attachments, adds it to the correspondence history, and records who reviewed it and when.
4. Or select **Reject**, which deletes the quarantined copy. A rejection cannot be undone from this screen.

Under **More**, **Create an expense draft from a receipt attachment** turns an emailed receipt into a draft expense for review.

If a forwarded message does not appear, confirm that the recipient address matches the matter's current address, allow a few minutes for delivery, and refresh the section. Ask an administrator to check delivery before rotating the address, and give them the address you were using.

Sent messages that failed or whose delivery is unconfirmed are marked **Delivery failed — review before retrying** or **Delivery unconfirmed — check Sent Items before retrying**. Check the sending mailbox before you send again.

## Search historic firm files

Open [Firm Memory](/firm-memory) to search the document sources your firm has authorized for you. In the unified research rollout, start with the query: **All authorized sources** is the default, and a matter is an optional filter rather than the entry gate. You can narrow the search to on-premises file shares or cloud sources, then filter by source, share, provider, file type, modified date, or matter. Results keep the source label and zero, one, or many linked matters visible.

For an on-premises result, use the relative location and local-index freshness to confirm the file and **Copy path** when needed. A stable LawHand result link appears only when the server can re-resolve the document under your current authorization; otherwise the card says the link is unavailable. **Open on this computer** is enabled only when the approved File Opener handoff is available; LawHand does not turn an agent-supplied path into a raw browser `file://` or `smb://` link. Cloud results open the provider's validated HTTPS page.

Coverage is part of the result. **Ready** means all authorized sources in that search reported complete coverage. **Partial**, **Indexing**, **Stale**, **Offline**, **Search unavailable**, or **Access unavailable** means the visible results are not the complete authorized corpus. LawHand says **No matching documents** only for complete ready coverage; otherwise it says **No matches in available sources**.

When unified research is unavailable, LawHand shows the matter-required file-share search instead. Select the matter first; LawHand limits the query to folders explicitly bound to that matter. The rollout never widens your source or matter authorization.

The matched text is retrieval evidence, not an instruction or a verified legal conclusion. Review the original file and cited page before relying on it.

## Collaborate on research

On the matter's Overview, select **Research** in Quick Actions to open the matter's **Research Workspace**. It keeps issues, searches, authorities, highlights, exclusions, outline items, and memo notes in a shared trail, and **Back to matter** returns you to the record.

1. Enter a title for the issue set, for example "Motion to compel research", and select **Create workspace**.
2. Add research records as you work and select **Save record**. Preserve the label on every item: **cited** means it has the linked source, **verify** still needs confirmation, and **model** is machine synthesis.
3. Select **Freeze snapshot** when you reach a milestone. Snapshots are immutable; **Export review package** downloads one for review.

A snapshot or export preserves labels, source links, and stored currentness or treatment warnings. It does not certify good law, Bluebook correctness, or complete provider coverage. Review the exact source, and have the assigned reviewer resolve any warning before relying on it.

## Check a litigation brief

On the matter's Overview, select **Brief Check** in Quick Actions.

1. Under **Run a Brief Check**, choose the brief (DOCX or PDF) and, optionally, the opposing brief.
2. Select **Run review**.
3. Work through **Citations**, **Quotations**, and **Omitted-authority candidates**, recording an attorney decision on each item.
4. Export the **Report** or the **TOA** (table of authorities draft) only after reviewing the original authorities and pin cites.

The review records citation normalization, accessible-source matches, pin and quotation evidence, bounded supporting or contrary-authority candidates, and unresolved or ambiguous items. It never decides that an authority is good law from an absence of negative evidence, and it preserves unknown and unavailable states when a source, provider, or currentness signal cannot be verified. Processing is bounded to 15 MB and 300 PDF pages (1.5 million extracted characters). Previous checks stay listed under **Previous checks**, and **Back to matter** returns you to the matter's documents.

## Client portal uploads

When your administrator has connected customer-owned cloud storage, portal files go to the matter's `client_uploads` folder in that datastore. The original submission stays there as the intake copy. Renaming its category does not move it.

If you revise, redact, or promote a client upload into work product, save the reviewed result as a new matter document in the appropriate documents, pleadings, or correspondence folder. This preserves the provider link and history of the original. If the customer cloud is unavailable, the upload fails and can be retried; a cloud-bound firm never receives a success message for a file saved only on LawHand infrastructure.

## Close a matter

When work is complete, select **Close matter** on the record, or drag the matter's card to **Closed** on the board.

1. LawHand checks what is outstanding. Items under **Resolve before closing**, such as unbilled work or a trust balance, must be dealt with first.
2. Items under **Outstanding, but not blocking** can be accepted: tick **I want to close this matter with these items outstanding**.
3. Add an optional **Closing note**, for example "Settled and disbursed; file retained per policy".
4. Select **Close matter**.

Closing cancels outstanding client follow-ups and voids signature requests that are still with a signer. It can be reversed with **Reopen matter**.

Leave enough context for the next person. Do not delete or overwrite history to make a record look tidy; if information is wrong, correct it in the supported field.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> If a document contains unusually sensitive information, follow your firm's handling policy before uploading or sharing it. LawHand permissions complement that policy; they do not replace professional judgment.

## Troubleshooting

| What you notice | Likely cause | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A matter is missing from **My Matters** | You are not on the matter team | Check **All accessible matters**, or ask the responsible attorney to add you under **Matter settings → People**. |
| Search finds nothing inside documents | Fewer than three characters, or the document has not been read yet | Type at least three characters; a new upload is indexed shortly after it is first read. |
| **Close matter** is disabled in the dialog | Something under **Resolve before closing** is still open | Resolve the listed item, such as unbilled time or a trust balance, then try again. |
| A forwarded email never arrives | Wrong or rotated address, or delivery delay | Compare the address with the one under **Forward email to this matter**, wait a few minutes, and refresh. |
| **Download** says the document must be reconciled | The document was changed in Word or Google Docs after it was last saved in LawHand | Select **Bring back changes** on its row. |
| **Open in Word** or **Bring back changes** asks you to reconnect | The firm's Microsoft 365 or Google connection has expired | An administrator reconnects it under **Administration → Integrations**. Meanwhile, use **Download** and **Upload revised**. |
| **Scan now** reports a failure | Your mailbox is not connected or needs reconnecting | Connect your account from [Calendar](/calendar) with **Connect Calendar**, then scan again. |
