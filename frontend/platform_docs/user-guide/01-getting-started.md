# Start here

LawHand brings matters, tasks, deadlines, communications, time, documents, and approved AI tools into one signed-in workspace. This chapter is a short tour: where things live, how to get help from any screen, and a first-day checklist you can follow in about fifteen minutes.

What you see depends on the modules your firm has enabled, your role, and the work assigned to you, so your screen may show fewer items than the pictures here.

## Your workspace at a glance

![The LawHand workspace: the navigation on the left, the Guide button in the top bar, and the customize and profile buttons at the bottom of the navigation](/guide-assets/workspace-tour.webp "The workspace, with the four controls you will use most")

1. **Navigation.** Every area you can use is listed on the left. The first group holds **My Matters**, **Assistant**, and **Firm Memory**. **Workspace** holds everyday tools such as Calendar, Tasks, and Template Studio. **Accounting** holds invoices, trust, and reports when your role allows them, and **Firm** holds **Add-on Modules**. Administrators and accountants also see **Administration**.
2. **Guide.** The **Guide** button in the top bar opens the part of this guide that explains the screen you are on. On a phone it is the book icon.
3. **Customize navigation.** The gear at the bottom of the navigation lets you hide items and change their order. See [Make the navigation your own](#make-the-navigation-your-own).
4. **Profile.** The person icon opens [your profile](/profile), where you manage your professional context, privacy, and connected assistants. The arrow beside it signs you out.

The top bar also has a **My Matters** shortcut, and an **Admin** shortcut for administrators and accountants.

> [!TIP]
> Collapse the navigation with the panel button next to the LawHand logo when you need more room, or drag the thin divider to its right to make it narrower or wider. Your choice is remembered on this device.

## Your first fifteen minutes

Work through these once, in order. Each step links to the screen it describes.

1. Open [My Matters](/matters). Confirm that the matters you work on appear in **My Matters**, and look for the red **needs attention** link under the heading.
2. Open [Tasks](/tasks). Check the **Overdue** and **Due Today** sections, and complete or reassign anything that is not yours.
3. Open [Calendar](/calendar). If you see **Connect Calendar** in the top right, connect your own Microsoft or Google calendar so your deadlines appear there too.
4. Open [your profile](/profile). Check your name and role, then fill in **Professional context** (role, job title, office, and primary jurisdictions) and select **Save context**. The assistant uses this context in general chats.
5. Still on your profile, read **Protect private details** and **Workspace MCP assistants** so you know how your account shares information with AI tools.
6. Come back to this guide from any screen with the **Guide** button, or from the **User guide** card on your profile.

## Get help on any screen

- Select **Guide** in the top bar. The guide opens at the section for the screen or panel you are using.
- Most chapters start with **Open in LawHand** buttons that take you straight to the screens it describes, so you can read a step and then do it.
- Use **Search this guide** at the top of the chapter list. Search matches words anywhere in a chapter, so try the label you see on screen, such as `Needs review` or `Close matter`.
- If you are still stuck, see [How to get help](/guide/account-safety-and-support#get-useful-help) for what to include in a request.

## Make the navigation your own

You can hide functions you never use and put the ones you use most at the top. Your layout follows your account to every device.

1. Select the gear (**Customize navigation**) at the bottom of the navigation.
2. Clear the checkbox next to any function you want to hide.
3. Use the up and down arrows next to a function to move it.
4. Select **Save layout**. Select **Cancel** to leave without changes.

To start over, open **Customize navigation**, select **Reset to role defaults**, then **Save layout**.

> [!NOTE]
> Hiding a function only tidies your navigation. It does not change what you are allowed to do, and your role still decides which functions are available to you. Once you reorder items, the navigation shows them as a single **My workspace** list instead of the usual groups.

On a phone, the bar at the bottom of the screen shows up to five shortcuts, and the menu button in the top left opens the full navigation.

## Why something might be missing

Access in LawHand is intentional. A missing menu item usually means the module is not enabled for your firm or role, or that your administrator configured a simpler view for your role. A matter may be missing because it has not been assigned to you.

Ask your firm administrator to review your access when:

- a colleague can see a workspace tool that you cannot;
- an expected matter is not listed;
- a feature says that a license or upgrade is required; or
- an integration shows as disconnected.

If your firm is on a limited plan, **Explore the full platform** at the bottom of the navigation describes what an upgrade adds.

> [!WARNING]
> Do not share accounts or ask someone else to work under your sign-in. Activity, approvals, time entries, and AI actions all need a reliable record of who did what.

## A useful working rhythm

Start the day with [Tasks](/tasks) and [Calendar](/calendar), do client work from the relevant matter in [My Matters](/matters), and record time while the context is fresh. Keep communications and documents inside the matter whenever possible so the file tells one coherent story.

## Troubleshooting

| What you notice | Likely cause | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A menu item a colleague has is missing | Your role or your firm's plan does not include it, or you hid it | Open **Customize navigation** and check the list. If it is not there, ask your administrator. |
| The navigation shows **No functions shown** | Every function is hidden in your layout | Open **Customize navigation**, select **Reset to role defaults**, then **Save layout**. |
| A matter you work on is not in **My Matters** | You are not assigned to it | Look under **All accessible matters** on the same page, or ask the responsible attorney to add you to the matter team. |
| The **Guide** button is missing on a screen | That screen has no guide section yet, or it belongs to Administration and you do not have access to the Admin Guide | Open the guide from your profile and search for the screen name. |
